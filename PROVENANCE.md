# Provenance

The released archive is tag `v1.1.1` of
https://github.com/zhouyi-xiaoxiao/prescribed-reaction-time-modes.  The
source commit it was built from is recorded by the builder as
`release_commit` in `environment/reference_platform.json` (together with
`release_date` and `release_version`) and as `commit` in `CITATION.cff`; the
tag is placed on the commit that records these receipts.  The builder
(`reproduction/build_reproduction_archive.py` in the source repository)
refuses to build a release unless every archived input is committed and
unmodified at that source commit.  File hashes are listed in
`MANIFEST.sha256`.

## Fixed-budget campaign (2026-09, new in v1.1.0)

All records under `artifacts/data/exact_m_fixed_budget/` were produced in
September 2026 by the scripts shipped in `code/` (records name their script;
ensemble index records also store their completion time) on the workstation recorded under `fixed_budget_campaign` in
`environment/reference_platform.json` (Apple M4 Pro, CPython 3.9.6, NumPy
2.0.2, SciPy 1.13.1, Matplotlib 3.9.4; the versions were read from that
interpreter when this release was prepared).

- **Path ensembles.**  Every exact-law estimate is an average over an
  ensemble of unkilled Euler--Maruyama paths built by
  `code/exact_m_prr_fk_exact_law.py`.  The campaign seed is `20260923`
  (`BASE_SEED`); each ensemble has its own stream tag and replicate index,
  and chunk `i` of an ensemble uses `SeedSequence(seed_entropy).spawn(n_chunks)[i]`
  with the entropy stored in full in its index record
  `fk_ensembles/<name>.json` (field `seed_entropy`, generator named in `rng`).
  The same index records store the model specification, the time grid, the
  per-chunk path counts and the SHA-256 and size of every original chunk file.
  The chunk files themselves (about 16 GB) are not archived.
- **Derived records.**  The item drivers (`fb_n1_*` to `fb_n9b_*`,
  `exact_m_prr_fk_n0_validation.py`, `fb_referee_checks.py` and the theory
  checks) read the ensembles and write the JSON records of their item
  directory; stochastic side runs (for example the direct-kill confirmations
  `N3/dk_*.json`, the Gaussian-tilt bridge checks `TH6/*.json` and the stream
  integrations I1--I5) store their own seed entropy, typically
  `[20260923, <stream tag>, ...]`.  The single-particle control
  (`exact_m_prr_upgrade/w6_single_particle/`), the seed repeats and the
  release-time smearing reuse the direct-kill simulator of release `v1.0.0`
  and record seed and stream tag in each file.
- **Theory scout.**  `notes/gap_diagnosis_20260923/theory_scout/analysis_*.json`
  are outputs of `analyze_fk.py` on scout path caches generated by
  `fk_paths.py`, `fk_bop.py` and `fk_var.py` with integer seeds 11--16, 21--24
  and 31--32 (arguments in `README.md`).  The caches are not archived.
- **Publication figures.**  `code/fb_make_paper_figures.py` draws every
  data figure of the article and the Supplementary Material from the records
  above and writes `artifacts/figures/fb_paper_figures_manifest.json` (the
  checks it performs, the SHA-256 of every output, and the names under which
  some figures are included in the manuscripts).  The builder verifies that
  every figure included by the manuscripts is byte-identical to the archived
  file.  The remaining numerical figures of the Supplementary Material are
  written by their item drivers (named in `RESULTS_SUMMARY.md`).
- **Closing campaign.**  The interval-arithmetic certificates
  (`fb_th8_interval_certificate.py`, `fb_c1_preparation_count.py interval`),
  the quadratures (`fb_p5_step6_tail.py`, `fb_n14_unimodality.py`,
  `fb_n14_fix_checks.py`), `fb_derived_numbers.py` and
  `fb_graphical_abstract.py` draw no random numbers.  The re-analyses
  `fb_th8b_fold_normal_form.py` (N9b ensembles, tag 87),
  `fb_c1_preparation_count.py fk` (N4 ensembles, tag 83),
  `fb_n13_third_order.py` (the N2 paths, tags 80 and 81, regenerated bit for
  bit from their stored entropy) and the FK side of
  `fb_n10_largeB_validation.py` (ensembles of tags 81 and 80) consume no new
  seeds.  New stochastic runs use the campaign seed `20260923` with these
  stream tags: 90 for the N10 direct-kill runs, the N11 FK evaluations and
  the P5 switching-function Monte Carlo; 91 for the N11 direct-kill runs, the
  N12 importance-sampled runs and the P5 gate-law Gaussian samples; 92, 93 and
  94 for the N12 direct-kill runs, halved-step runs and plain-FK diagnostic;
  97 for the N14 demos and general Monte Carlo.  Tags 90 and 91 are thus
  shared between drivers, but each record's full `SeedSequence` entropy also
  contains driver-specific fields (cell parameters, walker or path counts,
  chunk layout, grid step), and no stored tag-90 or tag-91 entropy vector
  occurs in two different drivers' records (checked when this release was
  prepared); each record stores its own entropy, which is authoritative.
- **General-transport counterexamples (new in v1.1.1).**
  `GX_counterexamples/checks.json` was written on 2026-09-24 by
  `fb_gx_counterexample_checks.py` on the same workstation with CPython
  3.14.6, mpmath 1.4.1 and NumPy 2.5.3 (a separate environment used for the
  mpmath checks).  Parts 1--4 (covariance identities, 60-digit
  outward-rounded interval enclosures with rational inputs, spiral
  constants) and the free-envelope grid counts draw no random numbers; the
  finite-noise illustration of part 5 uses `SeedSequence([20260924, 61,
  tag])` in one process (seed and stream tag distinct from those above).
  Before this release the record was regenerated so that every exported
  enclosure is rounded outward (the earlier, unreleased run printed
  round-to-nearest endpoints); apart from these strings, the rounded-down
  action-gap bound, a note and the run times, the regeneration reproduced the
  earlier run exactly, including the seeded illustration.
- **Frozen-gate checks (new in v1.1.1).**  `GB2_frozen_gate/checks.json` was
  written on 2026-09-24 by `fb_gb2_frozen_gate_checks.py` on the same
  workstation with CPython 3.14.6, mpmath 1.4.1 and NumPy 2.5.3.  Part 1 uses
  Python's `random.seed(20260924)` and parts 4a--4b NumPy's
  `default_rng(20260924)` in one process; the other parts draw no random
  numbers.  The script merges two development checks of the same day; its
  run reproduced their outputs exactly.
- **Manuscripts.**  The compiled article, Supplementary Material and
  graphical abstract are not archived (no public preprint; the article will be
  linked from `README.md` on publication).  The builder reads the compiled
  PDFs in the source repository and checks that both are newer than their
  LaTeX sources, that their logs report no undefined reference, that the
  Supplementary Material contains the Lean chapter and that the article cites
  this release; it refuses a tree that contains a manuscript PDF.

## Large-scale checks on Isambard 3 (2026-09, new in v1.1.0)

The records under `artifacts/data/exact_m_fixed_budget/HPC_*/` were produced
on the Isambard 3 supercomputer (GW4/Bristol; partition `grace`, aarch64
nodes with 144 cores; CPython 3.11.15, NumPy 2.0.2, as recorded in the `env`
field of every summary and under `hpc_campaign` in
`environment/reference_platform.json`) by the drivers `code/fb_hpc_*.py`,
submitted with the job scripts in `code/hpc_jobs/`.  Eight node-jobs were run
(one validation job cancelled at start-up, five simulation jobs, the census
analysis and one deterministic re-analysis); the per-job accounting and totals are in
`HPC_jobs/hpc_jobs_accounting.json`.  Only the reduced summaries were fetched
from the cluster; the per-chunk accumulators were not.  The assessments
`HPC_N5full/hpc_n5full_assessment.json` and
`HPC_N3full/hpc_n3full_assessment.json` and the figures
`artifacts/figures/fb_hpc_n5full.pdf` and `fb_hpc_n3full.pdf` were computed on
the workstation by `code/fb_hpc_n5n3_report.py` from the fetched summaries and
the workstation records `N5/n5_dt_ladder.json` and `N3/n3_summary.json`,
without random numbers.

Seeds: campaign seed `20260923` with stream tags 95 (time-step ladder), 96
(contact factorial), 97 (design-cell direct kill) and 98 (census); each driver
documents its entropy layout at the top of the script, and each record stores
its entropies.  Tag 97 is also used by the universality demos and general
Monte Carlo of N14; the two entropy layouts differ in their fourth word (the
peak count `m <= 5` for the direct kill, the scaled stripe width
`eps x 10^9 >= 2.5x10^7` for N14), so the streams are distinct.

In the archived job scripts, the cluster locations of the repository clone,
of its virtual environment and of the job-script directory (which sat at the
report root on the cluster) were replaced by `${PRR_ARCHIVE_ROOT}/code`,
`${PRR_ARCHIVE_ROOT}/code/hpc_jobs/` and `${PRR_PYTHON:-python3}` (see
`README.md`); the scripts are otherwise unchanged.  In
`HPC_jobs/hpc_jobs_accounting.json` the name of the temporary directory that
holds the operator notes (not archived) was replaced by the words "operator
notes (not archived)"; no number changed.

## Workstation paths

Stored records written on the workstation contained absolute paths (to the
path-ensemble store, to the repository, in two scripts to local input
directories, and in a few usage lines and one default cache directory of
closing-campaign scripts).  The builder rewrote these prefixes in the archived
copies:

| original prefix | archived as |
|:---|:---|
| the report directory of the source repository | (empty: paths become relative to the archive root) |
| the source repository root | `<source-repository>/` |
| the local build directory under the user's home (ensemble and cache store) | `<local-ensemble-store>/` |
| any other path under the user's home | `<home>/` |
| a temporary working directory | `<scratch>/` |
| the absolute path of a Python interpreter in a usage line | `python` or `python3` |
| cluster locations in the Isambard 3 job scripts | `${PRR_ARCHIVE_ROOT}/code...`, `${PRR_PYTHON:-python3}` |

The default `--cache` directory of `fb_n14_universality.py` was such a
temporary directory; in the archived script it reads `<scratch>/...`, so pass
`--cache` explicitly.  In the docstring of `fb_derived_numbers.py` the name
of that temporary directory was replaced by the words "temporary working".
Default locations
written as `~/.local-build/...` in scripts and comments are
generic defaults (for example of `PRR_FK_ENSEMBLE_ROOT`), not records of a
particular machine, and are left unchanged.

For every JSON and JSON-lines record the builder proves that only string
values changed; no number, key order or structure was altered.  Hence the
archived copies of the rewritten records and of the rewritten scripts
(`exact_m_prr_w6_width_diagnostic.py`, `exact_m_fb_th6_tilted_bridge_checks.py`,
`fb_derived_numbers.py`, `fb_n14_universality.py`, `fb_n14_general_mc.py`,
`fb_n14_unimodality.py`, `fb_n14_fix_checks.py`, `fb_p5_step6_tail.py`,
`fb_th8_interval_certificate.py`, `fb_th8b_fold_normal_form.py`,
`fb_gx_counterexample_checks.py` and
`notes/gap_diagnosis_20260923/theory_checks/contact_escape_check.py`) differ
from the committed sources by these path prefixes (and the docstring wording
above) only, while every other archived file is byte-identical to its source
file in the repository.  In the archived
`exact_m_prr_w6_width_diagnostic.py` the data directory becomes the relative
path `artifacts/data/`, so the script runs from the archive root; in the
archived `exact_m_fb_th6_tilted_bridge_checks.py` the directory of the
reduced-PDE inputs becomes `<local-ensemble-store>/...` because those inputs
were computed outside the source repository and are not archived (the script
then records them as missing; its archived records contain the results).

## Lean 4 package

`lean/formal_lean_prr/` is package `formal_prr` 0.2.0 as rebuilt on
2026-09-23.  Every file is copied from the package's own `MANIFEST.sha256`
and reproduces the hash listed there, with one exception: in the original
task brief `FORMALIZATION_TARGETS.md` (not a source file) the working-directory
prefix was rewritten as `<source-repository>/`, and its line in the archived
package `MANIFEST.sha256` was updated accordingly.  All `.lean` sources, the
lakefile, the lake manifest and the build and audit records are
byte-identical to the package.  The SHA-256 anchors quoted in the
Supplementary Material table of Lean modules are recomputed from the archived
sources at build time.  The default build target is the library `FormalPRR`
only; the companion library `FormalPRRCompanion` (39 declarations encoding the
arithmetic of a different manuscript) is archived because the historical
axiom records name its declarations, is imported by no `FormalPRR` module, and
is counted nowhere in the article.  The reference build log
(`lean_build_20260923_output.txt`), the axiom audit
(`audit_paper_20260923.lean` and its output) and `BUILD_RECEIPT.txt` describe
the archived sources; the files listed as historical in the package README
describe the `v1.0.0` package.

## Release v1.0.0 records (unchanged)

Every record under `artifacts/data/exact_m_offlattice_production/` and every
`v1.0.0` record under `artifacts/data/exact_m_prr_upgrade/` is unchanged from
release `v1.0.0` and was produced by the code shipped here on the platform
recorded by the top-level keys of `environment/reference_platform.json`
(recorded 2026-08-14).  Seeds are per campaign and are recorded in each JSON,
which is authoritative where this summary and a record differ: the 18
production rows (seventeen configurations and the `dt/2` twin) use seed
`20260808`; the W1--W5 upgrade campaigns use the campaign seed `20260813`
(`CAMPAIGN_SEED` in `exact_m_prr_upgrade_core.py`, mirrored as
`campaign_seed` in `exact_m_prr_upgrade/campaign_summary.json`) with
per-stream tags; the `dt`-halving comparisons use seed `20260819`; the
independent seed repeats use seeds `20260814`, `20260815`, and `20260816`;
and the three independent-seed runs at the halved time step
(`exact_m_prr_upgrade/robustness/dt_half_seed_repeats/`, stream tag 65) reuse
those three seeds.  The covariance-aware reclassification of all stored
records (2026-08-24;
`artifacts/data/exact_m_prr_upgrade/covariance_aware_reclassification.json`)
and the deterministic mean-field evaluation
(`exact_m_prr_upgrade/mean_field_boundary.json`) simulate no walkers.

Each stochastic JSON records the seed used to construct its NumPy
`SeedSequence`; upgrade and robustness records also serialize the stream tag.
The 18 earlier production JSONs omit the tag field, but their deterministic
driver fixes it to `1` and writes it explicitly for new reruns.  Independent
robustness seeds are separate simulations.  The `dt` and `dt/2` comparisons
use the same declared seed and tag, while `dt` itself enters the entropy, so
the paths are independent rather than coupled.

Two uncited pilot directories of the source repository
(`exact_m_prr_upgrade/w6_single_particle_pilot/` and
`exact_m_prr_upgrade/robustness/classifier_null_calibration/`, superseded by
`w6_single_particle/` and `exact_m_fixed_budget/I5_classifier_null/`) are not
archived.

If a persistent identifier is minted for this release, it is recorded in
`CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json`.
