# Release notes: v1.3.0

Release `v1.3.0` accompanies the version of the article submitted to
*Chaos, Solitons & Fractals*, **"Programmable multimodality and saddle-node
loss of reaction-time peaks under a fixed catalyst budget"** (the same work as
"Programming the number and weights of reaction-time peaks with a fixed budget
of static reactivity", retitled).  Its Supplementary Material is split into
SM-A (proofs of the main results), SM-B (numerical record) and SM-C (extended
proofs).  The release supersedes `v1.2.0` and keeps all of its code and
records unchanged.  No manuscript PDF is included.

## What is new

The code and records of SM-B Sections B16 and B17.  Paths below are relative to
`artifacts/data/exact_m_fixed_budget/`.

| topic (article) | records | script in `code/` |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Approach of the exact law to its weak-noise limits across the noise level at the eight anchor designs (m = 2, 3; equal and max--min allocations; B = 2, 8): basin masses against the first-order correction, the mean-field part, peak times, a time-step ladder at eps = 0.0125 (SM-B Section B16; figure `fb_v3_bridge`) | `V3_bridge/bridge.json` (fits, decomposition, time-step ladder, verdict), `V3_bridge/fk_index/*.json` (indexes of the sixteen new exact-law ensembles, with their seeds and SHA-256 hashes) | `fb_v3_bridge.py` |
| Independent recheck of the interval certificates of Proposition 4 with a second interval library, FLINT/Arb (python-flint), and a different method (Taylor jets, interval Newton, envelope identity): the anchors, the boxes of the two-stripe fold curve and the codimension-two switch (SM-B Section B17) | `V3_arb_recheck/recheck.json` (per-part agreement counts, negative controls, provenance with the SHA-256 of every input record) | `fb_v3_arb_recheck.py` |

New figure in `artifacts/figures/`: `fb_v3_bridge` (PDF and PNG).

## Seeds and determinism

The new exact-law ensembles of `fb_v3_bridge.py` use stream tag 301 with the
base seed `20260923`: twelve ensembles of 1e6 unkilled paths (m = 2, 3 at six
noise levels, time step 1e-3, 20 batch groups) and four ensembles of 2e5 paths
for the time-step ladder at eps = 0.0125; the full entropy tuple of each ensemble is in its index file
and in `bridge.json#new_runs`.  The ensembles at the same noise level and time
step share random numbers across m = 2 and m = 3; different noise levels or
time steps are independent streams.  The Arb recheck is deterministic; its box
sample uses `random.Random(20260926)` on the sorted box lists.

## Environment

`fb_v3_bridge.py` ran with CPython 3.9.6 and the packages of
`environment/requirements_fixed_budget.txt`.  `fb_v3_arb_recheck.py` needs
python-flint (the record was made with CPython 3.14.6, python-flint 0.9.0 and
FLINT 3.6.0, 256-bit midpoints); it imports nothing from the mpmath
certificates and reads only their JSON records.

## Not archived

The sixteen new path ensembles (unkilled paths) are regenerated by
`python3 fb_v3_bridge.py simulate` from the recorded seeds; only their index
files and the derived records are archived, as for the earlier ensembles.
